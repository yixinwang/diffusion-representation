# Frozen observed-feature optimizer comparison

This additive CPU study compares the unchanged original Frank–Wolfe fitter with the feasible similar-triangles accelerated fitter. It measures empirical optimization on the existing positive-spline family. It performs no generation, true-density evaluation, population validation, or real-data access.

The 36 cells are the Cartesian product of worlds `local,distant`, dimensions `8,32`, fitting-array counts `256,1024,4096`, and seeds `8101,8102,8103`, in that order. The runner imports the original positive-spline validation simulator and calls its unchanged purpose-1 stream. It uses the same graph and group assignments, sites in ascending order, and concatenation of whole-array observations for each shared head. Observations and each head's context/response arrays are preserved and hashed. Only observed contexts and responses are passed into the fitters. Simulator uniforms, inverse procedures, density functions, and coefficients are never fitter arguments. Pooled sites are dependent within an array; the original full-array count remains the statistical unit.

Both fitters use four bins, coefficient bounds 0.175 and 3.3, initialization at all-one coefficients, 500 updates, and the original Frank–Wolfe gap target 1e-4. Their feature maps, coefficient constraints, empirical negative log likelihood, and final gap definition agree. An equal update cap does not imply equal work or equal time. No cell, optimizer, iteration cap, tolerance, or hyperparameter changes in response to results. The accelerated fitter retains its feasible objective incumbent and independently recomputes that incumbent's final gap. The runner also independently recomputes objective, gap, and feasibility for each returned head and compares them to solver diagnostics. Final coefficients are saved before these checks.

The order is fixed: Frank–Wolfe then acceleration within each head. Each fit has its own feature construction and no coefficient warm start. Single-thread environment variables are mandatory. Timings include instrumentation and are single-run measurements with an explicit order/cache limitation. They support no unqualified runtime-superiority claim.

The runner counts feature construction, forward and transpose sparse products, column-sum passes, linear minimization, scalar root searches and their derivative calls, gradient/objective evaluations, projections, and projected rows. Original Frank–Wolfe line searches perform one endpoint derivative test per update in addition to the counted Brent callbacks. Its objective count is its recorded outer evaluations plus one new-objective evaluation per update. Accelerated counts are compared against its own diagnostics. Separate post-fit verification work and time are reported. Both fitters use the same transparent sparse-matrix wrapper; it delegates mathematical operations unchanged and captures the latest feasible likelihood-evaluation coefficients. This capture adds overhead and is distinct from the solver's returned incumbent.

Source identity requires the submitted full commit to equal HEAD and every listed source, test, original specification, and new specification/launcher to match its committed bytes before any observations are simulated. The output directory must be new. Completed heads, observations, coefficients, traces, operation counts, and input hashes survive later failures. Exceptions preserve the latest feasible evaluated coefficients when available, partial counts, elapsed time, and traceback. These coefficients may be an accelerated gradient point, not its objective incumbent. The final payload file hashes all preserved files. A scheduler warning requests partial preservation; forced termination or storage failure can still prevent a complete record.

The launcher requests one CPU, 4 GB, and 60 minutes. This allocation is a resource limit, not a replacement for the fixed per-head update cap. All 36 cells are required for a completed comparison. The CLI offers no reduced-study or tuning option. Direct function smoke checks may use fabricated tiny arrays and two updates before commit, but are software checks only. The actual study must wait for source review and commit.
