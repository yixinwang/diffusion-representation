# Independent review of the direct trapezoid prototype

Reviewed reference.py SHA25699784d08f559636da143551488039f6dad76877d74454b3a91d23b5939c86529. This review is for that pre-fix version. No fitting, model selection, observations or PSC work was performed. Independent checker/results are independent_check.py/.json in this directory.

The feature knots, heights, slopes and integrated knot values agree with the separately derived law. Exact rational moments in the earlier audit establish meanzero, second momentone and third momentzero. The conditional CDF normalizes at both endpoints and has derivative bounded between.325 and1.675. Four internal-CDF comparisons choose one of five segments. The rationalized quadratic inverse and the determinant signs are correct in exact arithmetic. The source first coordinate passes through unchanged; this prototype is a unit-square pair transform, not yet its complete Gaussian-source/root-model wrapper.

A genuine numerical endpoint defect remains in the reviewed version: decode(.873046875,1.,-.45) and decode(.373046875,1.,.45) reject with “inverse left unit interval.” Computing the last segment as its left knot plus a reconstructed offset can round aboveone even though the exact output isone. A grid of129 theta values and root-feature input values covering1,025 uniform points plus knots and neighbors produced1,869,210 inverse cases; eight theta batches contained endpoint failures. Failure examples and the exact source hash are preserved. No exceptions were suppressed or values clipped. Successful batches had CDF roundtrip error<=1.12e-16, exact reported inverse/forward LD cancellation in those calculations, and agreement with an independently coded inverse to3.33e-16.

Use exact flat-tail identities for the first and last segments: with d=1+kA, v=p/d on the first segment and v=1-(1-p)/d on the last. These are algebraic evaluations of the same ideal map, not clipping or a changed model. They avoid last-tail cancellation and map p=0/1 to exact0/1. Retain the quadratic inverse for the internal segments. Add regression cases for the examples above and their nextafter neighbors. Forward CDF evaluation can likewise use F(v)=d*v on the first segment and F(v)=1-d*(1-v) on the last. log1p(k*psi(v)) is preferable to log(1+k*psi(v)) for tiny dependence parameters.

The branch slopes and mathematical smoothness descriptions are already correct in the prototype: the conditional CDF is C1 as a function of v, but the two-variable transport is only piecewise differentiable because psi(u) has corners. The Jacobian identity is an a.e. statement; none of the tests establishes a globally C1 joint map. Quantization of extreme inputs remains a separate limitation even after stable endpoint formulas. Do not use the mathematical continuous KL theorem as a claim about the atomic finite-precision output law.

With the endpoint evaluation corrected and independently rechecked, the prototype is suitable to preserve as a tested scalar reference before a separately registered learning experiment. The reviewed pre-fix version should not be labeled boundary-complete solely from its nine original tests.
