## Independent finite-sample derivation

Let X=product of three independent reference-uniform features(2Vj-1). Then E0X=E0X³=0 and E0X²=1/27. For p_t=1+tX, E_t(27X)=t and Var_t(27X)=27-t². F(t)=E0[(1+tX)log(1+tX)] has F''(t)=E0[X²/(1+tX)], between1/[27(1+rho)] and1/[27(1-rho)]. Forward KL is the Bregman divergence F(t)-F(e)-F'(e)(t-e), yielding the stated54-denominator bounds.

With m=n/B independent roots per balanced cell, K groups are independent only conditional on each root. The site-averaged score has conditional variance at most27/K but common conditional mean theta(C). Thus the variance of its cell estimate is at most Var(theta(C)|cell)/m+27/(mK). Multiplying the root count by K would incorrectly remove the first term. Clipping the estimator to[-rho,rho] cannot increase squared error against any legal true theta. Averaging over an independent test root and training estimates gives MSE at most(1+1/m)A_B+27/(mK). Summing K triple KLs proves the stated full-observation upper bound. The balanced-root sampling assumption is essential; this is not an automatic bound for arbitrary iid roots/empty cells or dependent image patches.

For the sinusoid, A_B=a²/2[1-sinc²(pi/B)]. ## Additional implementation limitations

The fitted theta is a piecewise-constant function of the root. Its triangular transformation is normalized and invertible in exact arithmetic and differentiable almost everywhere, but generally **discontinuous** across context-cell boundaries. A fabricated legal two-cell model exhibits a follower jump about .06027 at c=.5. This is not a globally continuous learned flow; changing it to interpolation would require a new estimator/approximation analysis, not silently reusing this exact statement.

Two fabricated API probes also reveal incomplete finite-input validation: decode copies a NaN anchor to a nonfinite output without raising, and Model accepts NaN theta then log_prob returns NaN. The scalar inverse allows tiny excursions up to1e-14 beyond the unit interval before its outer sampler rejects endpoints; the model has no full Jacobian/logdet API. These are prototype limitations, not disprovals of the legal-input exact-real density or reported finite stress outputs. No code was repaired or promoted to production here. The20 original tests do not establish comprehensive nonfinite-input safety or global tail stability.

The construct plants public grouping and valid sharing; it learns neither an unknown graph nor a semantic representation. Native full-pipeline costs, strong trained latent-FM comparisons, and realistic image/video quality remain unestablished. Timing three sequential CPU repetitions with an identity chart supplies no general efficiency claim.
