# Binding negatives and limitations

- No real image/video superiority is established. The supplied three-seed cached
  pilot failed; the supplied later job stopped before quality. I quality remains
  unknown. No native model, PSC job, or video model is run by this package.
- This is not a second spline audit. No RQS validity term is diagnosed or repaired.
- Fixed-chart conditional total correlation, summary information loss, marginal
  non-Gaussianity, scale bounds and rank restrictions remain distinct obstacles.
  Learning a different full chart may change them, so the bounds are not universal
  lower bounds against learned invertible charts or latent generators.
- The positive toy plants a public local structure. It does not recover a hidden
  graph or a learned semantic representation. Learners do not receive teacher
  coefficients, signs, innovations, or a privileged chart.
- Pairwise distributions are all independent in the toy; its non-Gaussian triple
  dependence is the actual witness. Covariance alone would miss it.
- The alternating-sign condition makes every shared response no better than the
  product oracle. It is frozen in advance and is not repaired after evaluation.
- An equally cheap conventional tied triangular flow copies the candidate exactly.
  Therefore there is NO distinct architecture/mechanism advantage against that
  capable control. The positive claim is only over the explicitly restricted
  frozen-chart conditional-product family, and the estimation benefit of valid
  sharing also belongs to generic tied models.
- Moment fitting is appropriate for this explicit one-mode conditional density
  family, not a universally consistent copula learner. Binned context responses
  are nonparametric only as B grows; high context dimension is a genuine obstacle.
- Conditional independence across groups and correct site sharing are assumptions,
  not properties of native image/video residuals. Shared root variation is charged
  separately and is not reduced by multiplying the site count.
- Complete sampling costs at least O(D), plus chart/cached-map costs. CPU toy timing
  with identity chart establishes no native end-to-end efficiency.
- No empirical latent-FM/diffusion comparison is run. A finite-NFE comparison could
  support a finite-solver result only, not separation from all latent models.
- Invertibility is not a semantic-VAE guarantee. One decoded TRAIN clip is not
  video modeling. No contents are invented for the unavailable D3WM PDF.
- Repository and publication status must be read from the actual audit/delivery
  receipts. User-supplied native facts are not silently represented as independently
  verified source reads when repository access has not succeeded.
