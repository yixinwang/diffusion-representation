# Work-only central/tail tilt transport

`qalt/experiments/tilted_sampler_cost/candidate.py` implements the same scalar law as the frozen Pro8 reference, with no production integration or timing study. Domain: finite binary64 sources and e in [-.6,.6]; numerical range failures raise. Sources are never clipped or truncated. The |z|<=5 condition selects direct-probability arithmetic; its complement selects the original log-tail arithmetic.

Write s=-1 for z<=0 and s=+1 otherwise, p=Phi(-|z|), and b=1+s e. For the selected lower/upper transformed tail probability u, the stable root is `u=2p/[b+sqrt(b²-4s e p)]`. For lower tails this solves (1-e)u+e u²=p; for upper tails it solves (1+e)u-e u²=p. Then r=-s Phi^-1(u). The central branch evaluates p with ndtr and r with ndtri. The tail branch retains log_ndtr and evaluates log(u)=log2+logp-log(denominator) with ndtri_exp, so underflow of exp(logp) in the quadratic correction does not truncate the source probability. It retains the original reference's finite-precision treatment of that exponentially small correction.

The exact transformed density is `p_e(r)=phi(r)[1+e(2Phi(r)-1)]`. From `F_e(Q_e(z))=Phi(z)`, differentiation gives positive derivative `Q_e'(z)=phi(z)/p_e(r)`. Consequently the log absolute determinant is `(r-z)(r+z)/2-log1p(e(2Phi(r)-1))`. The difference-of-squares form avoids subtracting two separately large squared terms. This proves mathematical monotonicity and the density identity; finite-precision tests below are implementation checks, not an interval certificate.
