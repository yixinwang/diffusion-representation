# Primary-source check: analytic components

Read on 2026-09-09. This check establishes relevant precedents, not reproduced empirical performance.

- Shaw and Buckley, *The alchemy of probability distributions*, [arXiv:0901.0434](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.0434), equations (3), (4), and (11). The paper gives the quadratic CDF transmutation, analytic inverse, and normal-base density. Substitution `lambda=-e` gives exactly the scalar family used in the tilted-normal positive example. The distribution and analytic inversion therefore must not be claimed as new. The finite-learning catalog construction, restricted solver certificate, and measured implementation comparison require their own assessment.
- Lu and Huang, *Woodbury Transformations for Deep Generative Flows*, [arXiv:2002.12229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.12229). The primary abstract describes global interactions with efficient inversion and determinants through matrix identities. The proposed orthonormal conditional low-rank map belongs beside this precedent; cheap global flow mixing is not itself a new idea. We have not reproduced the paper's experiments.
