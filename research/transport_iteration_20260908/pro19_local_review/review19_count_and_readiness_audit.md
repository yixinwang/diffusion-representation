# Review19 inline proposal: independent count and readiness audit

Scope: integer parameter arithmetic and prospective source-level design review only. No proposed model was implemented, trained, or evaluated; current native study is unchanged. The proposal describes a **new** squeezed affine/spline flow, not the currently tested innovation-response I/P mechanism. No delivered implementation/source receipt is inferred from an inline architecture description.

## Counts are correct under explicit conventions

Assume every convolution/transposed convolution/linear has a bias, GN has trainable gain+bias, each codec residual block has two GN/SiLU/3x3-conv sublayers, and there are no other trainable projections/embeddings. Fixed64-dimensional time features have zero parameters. The flow's two-convolution conditioner has no GN. Its output head is1x1. FM has a final GN128 plus3x3 output convolution.

- One H coupling: conv6->128(3x3)=7040; conv128->128=147584; head128->12=1548; total156172. Sixteen give **2498752**.
- I adds6x23=138 raw scalar-spline parameters per layer: sixteen add2208, giving **2500960**. This requires separate parameters per layer/transformed channel, shared over all spatial sites and histories. If shared across layers too, the stated count would change.
- G replaces each12-output affine head with150 outputs:19350 head parameters, layer173974, total **2783584**. The150 channels must mean12 affine outputs plus6x(8width+8height+7internal-derivative) outputs.
- A codec residual block at width c has18c²+6c parameters. Summing the stated stem/down/up/head layers and six blocks yields encoder **1728272**, decoder **1744643**, total **3472915**.
- One FM block has two affine GN128=512, two biased conv128->128=295168, and FiLM linear256->256=65792, total361472. Time MLP64->256->256 adds82432. Pixel stem/output+finalGN yield **4427395** for12 blocks. Latent16-channel stem/output yield **3011472** for8 blocks.

The complete latent training pipeline therefore contains **6484387** parameters, not3011472. Prior+decoder inference has4756115 parameters if the encoder is absent and no other state is needed. Codec compression is3x32x32=3072 to16x8x8=1024 scalar coordinates:3:1 dimension reduction, despite4x spatial downsampling. The independent calculation and component table are in review19_parameter_arithmetic.py/.json; no framework model construction was needed.

## Mathematical and fairness gaps to close

**Specify exact transforms, not just counts.** Freeze squeeze/permutation convention, alternating6/6 channel masks, order of affine and RQS, scale function/bounds, spline interval/tails/minima/initialization, and every conditioner activation. If forward is affine then spline, inverse must undo spline then affine and logdet must add both. GN/FiLM/time embedding, padding/dilation cycle, skip ordering, output initialization and codec normalization/posterior/loss likewise need explicit definitions. Counts matching does not settle these choices. H must nest in I/G through identity splines; I's global per-channel knots should be embeddable in G via constant output biases.

**No inherited theorem of learned superiority.** H/I/G are standard finite coupling-flow families. A fixed spline shared over spatial sites/history may improve an affine-only member's fit on some data, but parameter economy does not prove better representation, optimization or end-to-end complexity. A capable G can emulate I. Affine compositions with nonlinear conditioners are already expressive; a simple one-layer conditional limitation is not a lower bound against the full16-layer learned chart. Nor do these counts prove an advantage over latent FM. Any conditional-KL argument must identify which fitted chart and conditional law are fixed and account for learning error, rather than assuming it away.

**Use the same observations.** Density-only dequantization while FM/codec fit different quantized targets changes the target distribution. Reuse the exact same recorded dequantized unit-cube arrays for every arm. Flow logit inputs and FM/codec affine rescaling to[-1,1] may be explicit fixed coordinate choices from those same arrays; preserve their inverse/preprocessing costs. Treat dequantized flow likelihood as continuous likelihood, not exact discrete likelihood, and do not fabricate a comparable exact FM NLL.

**Be honest about latent source dimension.** A deterministic codec+1024-dimensional FM is a conventional compressed generator but has1024 active Gaussian variables. Ignoring2048 padded coordinates does not make its distribution a full3072-dimensional density. Conversely requiring an extra stochastic3072-scale decoder only for fairness would change its intended model/cost. Declare the comparison's prior convention and actual active dimensions; the strict allD stochastic-latent comparison and this conventional codec comparison are different scopes. This deterministic codec is also not automatically a VAE or a test of VAE representation quality.

**Resolve learning-checkpoint accounting before execution.** With a final codec shared across all five latent checkpoints, a prior checkpoint at fraction f costs C_codec+f*C_FM, not f*(C_codec+C_FM). With40/60 allocation and zero overhead, those fractions at.2,.4,.6,.8,1 correspond to52%,64%,76%,88%,100% of total fitting cost. They are legitimate fixed-codec prior curves if labelled this way. They cannot be matched to five ambient checkpoints labelled20/40/60/80/100% as equal-cost results. For genuinely elapsed full-pipeline checkpoints, the codec and prior states must be those available at that time, including untrained-prior early points or a preregistered absence of samples. Future final codec weights cannot be used to represent an earlier pipeline state.

**Define what3000 seconds contains.** Clarify whether the40/60 split applies to a net fitting allowance after all construction,20 warmups,100 timed batch125 draws, five1000-sample/NFE64 evaluations, cache/transfer/checkpoint/numerical costs, or to an independently labelled fitting budget. Do not promise both3000 fitting seconds and3000 total seconds. Timing120 large64-NFE batches can be material; reserve from measured data-free shapes with a frozen rule, and fail rather than silently truncate/drop checkpoints. Five methods x three seeds x3000 seconds already imply12.5 hours of per-arm budgets, before any uncounted work. Parameter count is not an allocation or time guarantee.

**Training-loss plateaus do not qualify baselines.** A final10%-of-training loss median mixes noisy stochastic regression targets, approximation/optimization, and irreducible conditional variance. It is not a generation-adequacy test and should not gate a claim that a competent FM was beaten. Training and path losses are not comparable across codec/flow/FM objectives. Freeze sensible numerical/update/learning-curve diagnostics, permit a declared bounded fit-only baseline tuning allowance, retain failures, and evaluate all registered quality/compute points without repair-driven checkpoint selection.

## Minimal data-free qualification before any new real fit

1. Commit the exact architecture/protocol and verify framework parameter **and buffer/state** inventories against the arithmetic. Test one3072 input through the squeeze and all source coordinates, latent1024 shape, and exact checkpoint reload. Verify no unused registered trainable modules and explicit mode/dtype/device behavior.
2. For H/I/G, test nonidentity inverse/analytic logdet versus a tiny dense Jacobian, observed/source full32 round trips on fixed synthetic banks, all expected parameter/input gradients, shared-history masking, identity nesting and constant-output G emulation of I. Include real spline-knot neighbors, tails and nonfinite rejection. Existing reflected-kernel scalar qualification cannot stand in for this new whole-model qualification.
3. For codec/FM, test continuous time/FiLM shapes at t=0,1 and interior times, full output shape, unconditional source-only sampling, no observed-code context at generation, correct interpolation target/loss normalization and actual Heun NFE counter. Do not require an exact inverse from a generic finite-Heun FM; do require finite field/outputs before final pixel transform, strict checkpoint/state identity, and no hidden redraw/clipping.
4. Exercise a fabricated mini training/save/reload/failure cycle for codec and priors, including strict all-gradient presence, frozen codec during prior fitting, frozen evaluation, source/path/index RNG ownership, and derived latent-statistic caching from fitting inputs only. This is implementation smoke, not adequacy or quality evidence. Codec reconstruction quality and baseline learning adequacy remain real fit-only empirical obligations afterward.
5. On the intended device, use fixed synthetic shapes to measure initialization, peak memory, one forward/backward/update, sampling at the exact batch/NFE settings, and warmup/timing/evaluation overhead. Freeze a feasible complete-cost ledger, per-arm deadline behavior, partial-output validity rows, failure receipts, and checkpoint semantics before canonical loading. Record compilation/mixed-precision choices identically where applicable; no selected fastest backend from uncharged trials.

These are minimal prerequisite checks, not a new full experimental design. The fixed source-access failures and proposal provenance should remain in its eventual package. No native superiority, new-architecture novelty or finite-learning guarantee follows before the new model is specified and tested.
