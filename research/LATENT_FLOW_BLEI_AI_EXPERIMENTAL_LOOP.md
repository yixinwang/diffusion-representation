# Latent-Flow Standing Instruction: Blei AI Experimental Loop

Added 2026-08-27. This file is append-only project guidance. It supplements, and never supersedes, every existing objective, artifact, split, source pin, result, failure, disclosure, branch, commit, tag, manifest, and unopened-confirmation status in the latent-flow program.

## Verbatim standing instruction

Add this standing instruction to the latent-flow project; preserve all existing objectives, evidence, splits, files, and failures. Apply the user-requested Blei AI experimental loop: freeze final estimand, practical margin, headroom, strongest same-information baselines, independent units, strata, compute/tuning, development versus untouched confirmation, and stop rules before outcomes. State mechanism, rival, falsifier, recovery/impossibility conditions, and threshold or smooth-response prediction. Build the smallest mathematical model retaining every earlier success and failure; derive an exact target and substantial finite experiment-aligned advantage before adding a component; map the same intervention to the realistic nonlinear method with transfer error and failure conditions. Progress through deterministic, known-truth, controlled, realistic-development, untouched-confirmation, and replication stages. Attribute gain by B+S versus B+S+A with zero/random/frozen/shuffled and parameter/compute-matched controls under identical information and resources. After every weak or negative result, reproduce it, locate the first failed layer, preserve a rival diagnosis, state the failed mathematical premise, change one layer, derive the minimal checked algorithm and falsifier, and run the cheapest study that can reject it. Preserve all negative rounds and never tune on confirmation. Latent-flow-specific: A must be the latent-flow construction itself and must cause a large joint natural-image/video quality and measured training/inference efficiency gain over full models, positive-rate VAE controls, common compression/support, and matched generic alternatives.

## Binding experiment record

Before any outcome is inspected, every new round must freeze:

1. the final estimand and direction of improvement;
2. the practical margin and remaining baseline headroom;
3. the strongest same-information full, positive-rate VAE, common-compression/support, and matched generic baselines;
4. independent experimental units, clustering, strata, seed roles, and paired analysis;
5. data, prior, conditioning, tokenizer/chart access, splits, NFE, capacity, optimization, tuning, and total compute budgets;
6. development, untouched confirmation, replication, and stop rules;
7. the mechanism, principal rival, falsifier, recovery and impossibility conditions, and a threshold or smooth-response prediction.

The smallest mathematical model must retain every earlier success and failure relevant to the proposed intervention. It must yield an exact estimand and a substantial finite, experiment-aligned advantage before a new component is admitted. The corresponding realistic nonlinear method must state the transfer-error term and conditions under which the toy advantage can disappear.

## Required staged progression

Every claimed mechanism advances in order through deterministic checks, known-truth simulation, controlled experiments, realistic development, untouched confirmation, and independent replication. A later stage cannot repair leakage or a failed earlier stage by relabeling evidence.

## Attribution contract

Let `B` be the strongest fair base model, `S` the shared support, compression, chart, conditioning, and systems improvements supplied to all relevant arms, and `A` the latent-flow construction itself. The causal attribution comparison is `B+S` versus `B+S+A` under identical information and resources. Required controls include zero, random, frozen, shuffled, parameter-matched, compute-matched, and copied-mechanism/equivalence controls whenever applicable.

`A` cannot be a ranker, selector, post-hoc gate, extra information channel, privileged tokenizer, or unmatched support mechanism. Promotion requires that the latent-flow construction itself produce a large joint natural-image and real-video quality gain together with measured training and inference efficiency over converged full-dimensional models, positive-rate VAE latent controls, common-compression/support controls, and matched generic alternatives.

## Negative-result loop

After every weak or negative result:

1. reproduce the result under the frozen configuration;
2. locate the first failed layer in the deterministic → identification → optimization → generation → systems → evaluation chain;
3. preserve at least one serious rival diagnosis;
4. state the mathematical premise that failed;
5. change exactly one layer;
6. derive the minimal checked algorithm, predicted response, and falsifier;
7. run the cheapest study that can reject the revision;
8. preserve the entire negative round and never tune on confirmation.

## Preservation rule

No future round may silently replace, relabel, delete, average away, or omit prior positive, mixed, or negative evidence. This includes failed acquisitions, post-open repair disclosures, unsupported factorization routes, equivalence-control ties, memory/storage losses, unconverged-baseline caveats, and impossibility or no-feasible-small-revision boundaries.
