# Approach registry

| Family | Mechanism | Identifying structure | Strongest consequence | Blocking gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Static orthogonal FIQ | PCA/flow-label active subspace plus conditional block fiber | Covariance eigengap and residual block margin | Exact endpoint law on a linear-chart block-Gaussian class | Nonlinear chart recovery and image-scale chart cost |
| Nonlinear paired-invariance quotient | Reversible shear whose active coordinate is invariant across repeated futures | Repeated condition with connected stochastic support and a rank gauge | Nonlinear state recovery up to reparameterization | Repeated futures are privileged and not generally available |
| Moving-chart FIQ | Jointly conjugate ambient dynamics into triangular form | Population closure under a bounded-cost chart class | Pathwise parity and perturbation bounds | Chart discovery can hide the generator; finite selection is not certification |
| QALT | Apply a quotient and analytic/local fiber inside a fixed VAE latent | Held-out closure plus conditional fiber adequacy | Strict compute reduction at matched quality; restricted finite-step quality gap | Optimized same-information baselines tie quality |
| Commutant-spectral residual JBD | Recover an unordered residual block decomposition from conditional covariance contrasts | Within-block irreducibility, inequivalent blocks, feature-rank condition, and a positive commutant eigengap | Haar-rotation recovery up to within-block rotations and block swap | Dense charts and commutant operators do not yield image-scale efficiency |
| Wavelet/local QALT | Fixed invertible multiscale chart and local high-frequency fiber | Local conditional likelihood and active-token closure | Hardware-relevant image/video specialization | Must beat token pruning, sparse attention, exact affine fibers, and few-step solvers |

Blocked approaches are reopened only with a new identification mechanism or a theorem that covers the implemented chart and fiber.
