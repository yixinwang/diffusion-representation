`M_a(u)=((1-||a||^2)u+2(1+a^T u)a)/(1+||a||^2+2a^T u)`.

`p'=r M_a(u)`, `b_l'=b_l+U_l(p'-p)`.

The inverse replaces `a` by `-a`. The untouched orthogonal complement is retained exactly. The Euclidean log-Jacobian is

`(m-1){log(1-||a||^2)-log(1+||a||^2+2a^T u)}`,

because the radius is unchanged and the spherical map is conformal. The complete density is the frozen C4G density plus the two stage terms; no coordinate is deleted and no VAE/ELBO/reconstruction objective is introduced.
