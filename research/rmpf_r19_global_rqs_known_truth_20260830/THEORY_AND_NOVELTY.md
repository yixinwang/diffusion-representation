# Exact map, finite advantage, and novelty boundary

Split the frozen four-dimensional conditioner and target into two planes. Let `phi_c,j`, `phi_t,j` be their angles and let the target radii remain unchanged. In unchanged global state `s`, define

\[
m_s(\phi)=\beta_{s1}\sin\phi+\beta_{s2}\cos\phi.
\]

For a strictly increasing periodic rational-quadratic spline `R_s`, the data-to-base map is

\[
\eta_j=\operatorname{wrap}(\phi_{t,j}-m_s(\phi_{c,j})),\quad
\xi_j=R_s(\eta_j),\quad
\phi'_{t,j}=\operatorname{wrap}(\xi_j+m_s(\phi_{c,j})).
\]

The inverse uses `R_s^{-1}` with the same unchanged conditioner. Because planar area is `r dr dphi`, target radius is fixed, and the conditioner does not depend on the target angle,

\[
\log|\det DT|=\sum_{j=1}^2\log R_s'(\eta_j).
\]

The parent density plus this term remains exactly normalized. Identity splines and zero phase coefficients exactly recover the parent.

For an aligned teacher generated by the exact inverse map, strict propriety gives

\[
ES(Q_0;P)-ES(P;P)=\tfrac12\mathcal E(P,Q_0)>0.
\]

For a fitted law `Qhat`, a common-source coupling yields

\[
ES(Q_0;P)-ES(Q_{\rm hat};P)\ge \tfrac12\mathcal E(P,Q_0)-2W_1(Q_{\rm hat},P).
\]

No headroom exists when every phase-residual angle is uniform within state. The first alias boundary is context-dependent mode permutation or higher-harmonic conditioning, residual copula dependence between the two target planes, radial/orthogonal-complement dependence, or distinct contexts with identical phase-residual marginals.

## Novelty audit

The primitives are not novel:

- Durkan et al. (2019), *Neural Spline Flows*, introduced analytically invertible rational-quadratic spline couplings.
- Rezende et al. (2020), *Normalizing Flows on Tori and Spheres*, developed exact angular-manifold flows.
- Meng et al. (2022), *ButterflyFlow*, developed structured efficient invertible mixing.
- Draxler et al. (2024) established broad expressivity for well-conditioned coupling flows.

The only candidate contribution is the narrow combination of the frozen exact FSMLF/R7 scaffold, shared two-plane source-fit phase residualization, a statewise periodic RQ circle map, exact Cartesian likelihood accounting, compact parent fallback, and a practical proper-energy gate before real data. No broad novelty claim is made.
