# Claim ledger

| ID | Claim | Status | Required evidence | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | FIQ-FM exactly matches a full-dimensional endpoint law on a declared quotient--fiber class. | Proved, conditional | Endpoint disintegration proof and source preservation | Does not establish that natural images admit the class or that the chart is learned. |
| F2 | FIQ-FM has latent-order repeated computation. | Proved for repeated field only | Count active field, chart, fiber, and decoder separately | Dense charts can dominate total cost; image-scale structure remains a proposal. |
| F3 | An equal-active-dimension FIQ representation is better than a KL-VAE representation. | Restricted proof only | Common task/metric and a declared VAE decoder/rate family | No unrestricted VAE dominance is possible. |
| F4 | FIQ-FM matches full-flow quality on observed images. | Not established | Fresh multi-seed image benchmark with a sufficiently trained full-flow frontier | Existing digits data give mixed metrics and the original reproduction command was broken. |
| Q1 | QALT strictly improves quality over every latent flow or diffusion. | Closed | Counterexample: a full-latent baseline uses the same exact fiber update | An unrestricted latent class contains QALT. |
| Q2 | QALT is strictly cheaper at matched quality when a cheap fiber replaces repeated full-token processing. | Proved conditionally; proxy confirmed | Same realized active sampler and positive measured break-even margin | The oracle operation ratio is about 0.213, but latency, memory, chart selection, packing, fiber, decoder, and hardware behavior remain unmeasured. |
| Q3 | QALT strictly improves quality over a full-latent finite-step solver that integrates a solvable fiber inexactly. | Proved and oracle-confirmed for the restricted solver | Exact Gaussian KL and Wasserstein calculation | All finite-Euler gaps were positive; exact and split solvers tied quality exactly. |

| Q5 | QALT improves the information in a fixed VAE code. | Closed | A bijection preserves the generated sigma-field and mutual information | QALT may improve geometry for a restricted generator, not information. |
| X1 | Both methods significantly improve realistic image and video generation. | Not established | Registered observed-data studies, strong optimized baselines, sealed tests, repeated data and training units | No current artifact supports this breadth. |
| M1 | Multiscale QALT matches full-token endpoint quality with latent-order repeated work on a declared nonlinear/non-Gaussian image/video law. | Proved conditionally and procedurally confirmed | Exact chart/fiber theorem, optimized tie control, 30 fresh units, corrected latency and live-array accounting | Procedural coefficients and local kernels only; no learned neural sampler, observed data, FID/FVD, or GPU evidence. |
| M2 | Multiscale QALT is better than an equal-coarse-dimension VAE representation. | Restricted proof and procedural confirmation | Common chart/coarse law plus a fixed diagonal Gaussian decoder; conditional KL gap | An unrestricted VAE can use the same mixture fiber and tie. |
